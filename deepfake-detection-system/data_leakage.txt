## Data Leakage Discovery & Fix

### Problem Identified:
- Initial results showed 97% accuracy with perfect AUC (1.0)
- Discovered data leakage: train/val/test splits contained augmented versions of same base images
- Model memorized specific synthetic faces rather than learning real vs fake patterns

### Solution Implemented:
- Split data at video level before augmentation
- Ensured no shared source material between train/val/test
- Retrained model with proper methodology

### Results:
- More realistic 68% accuracy 
- Demonstrates understanding of proper ML validation
